Research Intern

Swiss Federal Institute of Technology Lausanne (EPFL) Jul 2023 – Sept 2023

I collaborated with the Machine Learning for Education (ML4ED) laboratory on a project that centered around enhancing the explainability of clustering. In this project, we leveraged student data to cluster their behaviors effectively. Our approach involved employing a discriminator neural network with a rigorous sparsity criterion to dynamically select a subset of crucial features for each student. Subsequently, we conducted experiments using clustering techniques on this reduced feature set and evaluated the interpretability of the resulting clusters.

Spain, Portugal & Paris

May 2026

My biggest trip abroad so far: 16 days across seven cities, planned from start to finish and taken with a close friend, the first time I left Brazil for Europe purely on vacation. It was also her birthday and her first trip abroad, which made sharing every one of these places with her even more special. We started in Porto, with its tiled facades and the Clérigos Tower, then Madrid’s Almudena Cathedral and Royal Palace, and on to Seville, the city I was most excited to visit, and it exceeded every expectation: the Moorish gardens of the Real Alcázar felt like walking straight into Game of Thrones, and the Plaza de España was as grand as I’d imagined. Barcelona was a delight too, with the Gothic Quarter and Park Güell, swimming in the Mediterranean again, and seeing the Sagrada Família from the inside. In Paris, Montmartre, my favorite neighborhood there, became my friend’s favorite as well. We closed the trip in Lisbon’s steep, lantern-lit streets and the fairy-tale Pena Palace up in Sintra.

Forró Dance

Forró Dance

Forró is the music and dance style I love most and know best. I have been dancing it for over four years, and I currently teach at Pé Descalço, a network of dance schools.

Forró is a genre of Brazilian music and partner dance that comes from the Northeast of Brazil, closely tied to the Festas Juninas held every June. It is traditionally played by a trio of accordion, zabumba drum and triangle. Today it is popular all over the country, with festivals, dance schools and competitions dedicated to it.

Torneio Forró Dança 2025, Itaúnas ES

July 2025

Forró is one of my favorite things to dance. In 2025 I competed with my partner Gabriel Galego at the Torneio Forró Dança (TFD) in Itaúnas, Espírito Santo, the biggest forró festival in Brazil, and we won first place in the amateur category.

The competition runs over three days. The first stage is choreography, and five pairs move on from it. The second stage is improvisation, which narrows the field down to three pairs. The final is improvisation again, danced to two songs drawn on the spot, one slow and one fast, and it decides the podium.
